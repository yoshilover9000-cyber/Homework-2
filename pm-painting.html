PM Painting Webpage

Project overview

I created a webpage for PM Painting to introduce the company and describe the outdoor services it provides. The page gives visitors a clear place to learn what the business does and what kinds of services are available.

Services and presentation

The page brings the company and its outdoor services together in one web experience. Its purpose is to help visitors get familiar with PM Painting and understand the services the company offers before deciding to learn more.
