Spotify Search Webpage

This group project is a website where users can log in to Spotify and search for their favorite tracks. It brings account access and music discovery together, giving users a way to look for tracks they enjoy. I was responsible for the page design as part of the team.

My contribution

My role focused on the visual design and layout of the page. I helped shape the experience users see when they visit the site and use its track-search functionality, contributing the design work alongside the rest of the group.
