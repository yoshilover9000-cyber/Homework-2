Financial Frameworks Partners Webpage

Project overview

I created a webpage for Financial Frameworks Partners to introduce the company and explain who they are. The goal was to give visitors a clear first impression and a straightforward place to learn about the organization.

Design and presentation

I worked on presenting the company through a dedicated web experience, organizing its introduction so visitors can understand the purpose of the page as they explore it. This project let me practice communicating information about an organization through webpage design.
